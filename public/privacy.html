Who Dis? Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Who Dis? is a small app by Swathi Sambasivam for cleaning up your Google contacts. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no Who Dis? server, and I never receive, see or store your contacts.

What the app accesses

When you tap Connect Google Contacts, you give the app permission to see, edit and delete your Google contacts. The app uses that permission only to:

  • show you your contacts as cards,
  • delete the contacts you swipe left on,
  • star or un-star favorites,
  • save the edits, tags and labels you make.

Where your data goes

Your contacts travel only between your browser and Google. The app sends nothing to me or to anyone else. It has no analytics, ads or trackers, and the site instructs your browser to refuse connections to anything other than Google's contacts service, Google Fonts and Google's photo host.

If you tap Messages, Gmail or WhatsApp on a contact, that one phone number or email address is handed to that app so it can open the conversation.

What is kept on your device

  • Your place in the deck: contact IDs and counts, not names, numbers, emails or notes.
  • Your tag list and display settings.
  • The Google sign-in token, for the current tab only. It expires after about an hour.

The app sets no cookies. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes everything above and does not touch your contacts.

Deleting and undoing

Contacts you delete are removed from your Google account. Google normally keeps deleted contacts in its Trash for 30 days, where you can restore them. Export a backup from Google Contacts before your first run.

Removing the app's access

You can remove Who Dis? at any time from your Google Account under Security, then third-party connections.

Use of Google data

Who Dis?'s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

A plain warning

I'm not a security expert, and Google has not reviewed this app. The code is public so anyone can check it.
